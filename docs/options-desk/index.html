Inspect the calculation & assumptions

What you are seeing

45 precomputed cases: three synthetic snapshots, five CSO volatility shifts, and three vanilla volatility shifts. Each case is evaluated independently by the original Python engine.

Selecting a new snapshot resets volatility to its market calibration. The sliders choose exact stored cases; they do not interpolate hedge quantities or run an online optimizer.

What this does not establish

No observed market data, historical hedge performance, executable quotes, or real-time connection is demonstrated. Limited-search solutions are feasible proposals, not proved optima.

The complete Python application supports arbitrary validated inputs and is available in the source repository.
